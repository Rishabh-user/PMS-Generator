# 2. GENERAL

Materials shall be as per ASTM, BS or API specifications as referred in relevant PMS sheet and in compliance with the COMPANY Functional Specifications. Materials shall be new and unused, clean and free from rust, pits and obvious defects. Material older than two years from the date of manufacturing shall not be permitted.

## 2.1. MECHANICAL REQUIREMENTS

### 2.1.1. MINIMUM THICKNESS

The minimum thickness of material shall be based on requirements of pressure and other mechanical loading. Minimum wall thickness of carbon steel pipes, including corrosion allowance, shall be as following:

1. DN 40 (1 1/2”) or less - schedule 80

2. DN 50 (2”) through DN 150 (6”) - schedule 80

3. DN 200 (8”) through DN 600 (24”) - schedule STD

4. Above DN 600 (24”) - schedule STD

### 2.1.2. CORROSION ALLOWANCE

All Carbon Steel material shall incorporate a minimum corrosion allowance of 3 mm. Stainless steels, copper nickel, GRE material, and other corrosion resistant materials shall be used without extra-thickness for corrosion allowance.

### 2.1.3. MILL TOLERANCE

The mill tolerance on wall thickness shall be minus 12.5 %.

## 2.2. MATERIAL IDENTIFICATION AND CERTIFICATION DOCUMENTS

EN10204 Type 3.1/3.2, shall be provided as applicable. All piping components material certification shall be traceable to heat numbers. Certificates, including all mill certificates, material certificates, mechanical test certificates, welding qualification certificates, heat treatment certificates and hydrostatic test certificates duly certified by COMPANY approved third party inspection agency shall be available at final inspection and for counter signature by the certification authority and stored for a minimum of 5 years after acceptance of the piping by the CONTRACTOR.

## 2.3. SOUR SERVICE REQUIREMENTS

This shall be in accordance with NACE Standard MR-01-75/ISO 15156-1/2/3.

## 2.4. BOLTS AND NUTS

All bolting shall comply with applicable ASTM standards, ASME B16.5, and ASME B16.47 series A requirements.

Bolting shall conform to ASME B1.1 and the relevant ASTM standards. Nuts shall conform to ASME B18.2.2. All stud bolts shall be class 2A fit and nuts shall be class 2B fit. Nuts shall be semi-finished, heavy, hexagon.

Threads for all bolts and nuts up to 1 inch diameter shall be unified coarse UNC series and 8 UN series for diameter 1 1/8 inch & above.

Hydraulic Bolt tensioning shall be carried out on all bolts 1.1/8-inch diameter and above. Bolting for use with bolt tensioners shall have the normal length increased by one bolt diameter.

All stud bolts and nuts shall be XYLAR 2 + XYLAN 1070 coated with minimum combined thickness of 50μm.
